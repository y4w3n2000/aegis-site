Services

Four core advisory services

For companies operating in, or entering, Southeast Asia.

01

Regulatory Strategy

Regulatory pathways for product approvals, market authorisations and compliance frameworks. Work includes pre-submission strategy, dossier preparation support, and managing agency relationships through the approval process.

02

Stakeholder Engagement

Representing client interests with regulatory agencies, ministries and intergovernmental bodies across the region, from formal submissions and stakeholder consultations to direct engagement on policy proposals.

03

Market Access

Advice on the policy and regulatory conditions for market entry across Asian jurisdictions, including licensing, import and distribution frameworks, and the government relations strategy needed to secure and maintain access.

04

Government Incentive & Investment Support

Identifying and pursuing incentive programmes, investment facilitation mechanisms and public funding opportunities for companies establishing or expanding operations in the region.

Sectors

Where technical complexity meets government scrutiny

Aegis works with companies in two sectors where policy shapes what can be built, approved and sold.

Healthcare

Regulatory frameworks for medical devices and health technologies vary significantly across Southeast Asia. Aegis advises on approval pathways, post-market obligations, and regulatory change across ASEAN markets.

Advanced Manufacturing

Semiconductors, industrial technology and precision equipment sit at the intersection of industrial policy, export controls and national security regulation. Aegis advises manufacturers and technology suppliers on government relations strategy in this environment.

The Practice

An independent public policy advisory practice focused on Asia

Aegis is built around direct familiarity with the region's regulatory agencies, legislative processes and policy dynamics, drawn from a career spanning public service, multinational healthcare and industry associations.

Aegis provides government affairs and public policy advice. It does not offer legal services.

Engage

Advising on market entry, regulatory approvals and policy engagement across Asia

Aegis works with government relations leads, regulatory teams and senior executives at multinational companies. Engagements typically begin with a confidential briefing.

Confidentiality

Response time

Government affairs and regulatory strategy for healthcare and advanced manufacturing. General legal services are not offered.
